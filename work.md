<section id="research">
  <h2>Research</h2>
  <p>I am looking for a PhD place or a joint research project in brewing, distilling or wine science, where process data and AI meet. My questions come from the plant floor. How do you turn the messy records a brewery already keeps into reliable process insight? How do you forecast fermentation and maturation with honest uncertainty? Where does AI help a quality team and where does it mislead it?</p>
