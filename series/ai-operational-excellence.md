Every beverage plant is now being offered AI, and most of the offers use the same four words without defining any of them. This series starts from the beginning. It explains what AI, generative AI, agentic AI and AGI actually are, in plain language and with plant-floor examples. Then it sets out the operational excellence vocabulary every data person needs, and shows where each kind of AI genuinely helps: on the filler, the pasteuriser, the CIP set and the shift handover book.

It is written for plant managers who want to cut through the pitch, and for data people who want to speak the plant's language. It covers breweries, wineries, distilleries and the packaging lines they share.

## The series

1. **[What Is AI, Really? The Ladder From Rules to AGI, Told on a Beverage Plant Floor]({{ '/2026/what-is-ai-ladder-rules-to-agi-beverage-plant/' | relative_url }})**: rules, machine learning, deep learning, generative AI, agentic AI and AGI, one rung at a time.
2. **[What GenAI and LLMs Actually Are: Tokens, Context, Hallucination and RAG, Explained Through a CIP Log]({{ '/2026/what-is-genai-llm-tokens-rag-cip-log/' | relative_url }})**: how a language model works, why it makes confident mistakes, and how retrieval fixes most of them.
3. **[What Agentic AI Is: Tools, the Plan-Act-Observe Loop, MCP and Five Levels of Autonomy]({{ '/2026/what-is-agentic-ai-tools-mcp-autonomy-levels/' | relative_url }})**: how agents use tools, and the L0 to L4 ladder for deciding what they may do alone.
4. **[Operational Excellence Basics for Data People]({{ '/2026/operational-excellence-basics-oee-for-data-people/' | relative_url }})**: OEE with a worked filler-line example, the six big losses, TPM, lean, SPC and where plant data lives.
5. **[Classic AI for OpEx]({{ '/2026/classic-ai-opex-predictive-maintenance-soft-sensors-spc/' | relative_url }})**: predictive maintenance, soft sensors, anomaly detection on top of SPC, and energy and water against a fair baseline.
6. **[GenAI for OpEx]({{ '/2026/genai-opex-shift-handover-sop-rag-root-cause/' | relative_url }})**: shift handovers, SOP answers, root-cause support, one-point lessons and a multilingual floor.
7. **[Agentic AI for OpEx]({{ '/2026/agentic-ai-opex-oee-agent-cmms-guardrails/' | relative_url }})**: an OEE loss agent that drafts work orders, and the Purdue-model guardrails that keep it off the PLC.
8. **[The AI for OpEx Roadmap, and Where It Breaks]({{ '/2026/ai-opex-roadmap-where-it-breaks-agi-hype/' | relative_url }})**: data first, pilots by loss value, honest ROI, change management and the AGI hype.

Read them in order: the first three build the AI vocabulary, the fourth builds the plant vocabulary, and the last four put them together.
