A distillery is two businesses with two clocks. The still house runs in hours, with physics that has been understood for a century. The warehouse runs in years, with wood, weather and a nosing glass as the final judge. AI behaves very differently in each, and most of what gets pitched to distillers ignores the difference.

This series takes one problem at a time, shows the data engineering that makes it reliable, and puts GenAI in the one role it is actually good at: understanding the question, calling tested tools and explaining the answer.

## The series

1. **[A Physics-Informed Digital Twin of a Pot Still: Why Pure ML Twins Drift]({{ '/2026/physics-informed-digital-twin-pot-still/' | relative_url }})**: Rayleigh distillation, alcohol conservation, and a hybrid model with physics for structure and data for parameters.
2. **[Soft Sensors and Proactive Bands Instead of Alarms at the Spirit Safe]({{ '/2026/soft-sensors-proactive-bands-spirit-safe/' | relative_url }})**: strength between hydrometer readings, bands built from good runs, and fewer alarms rather than more.
3. **[An LLM Copilot for the Still Operator: RAG Over SOPs, Read-Only Tools, No Setpoints]({{ '/2026/llm-copilot-still-operator/' | relative_url }})**: procedure search with citations, handover drafts, and the safety of the tool that does not exist.
4. **[Cask Inventory as Data Engineering: SCD2 Positions, Regauge Events and Angel's Share as a Derived Number]({{ '/2026/cask-inventory-data-engineering-scd2/' | relative_url }})**: twelve years of history kept, and loss calculated rather than stored.
5. **[A GenAI Blending Assistant With the Maths Done in Code]({{ '/2026/genai-whisky-blending-assistant-lp/' | relative_url }})**: a linear programme for the vatting, density tables for the water, and the age statement as a hard rule.
6. **[Where Distillery AI Breaks: A Twelve-Year Feedback Loop]({{ '/2026/where-distillery-ai-breaks/' | relative_url }})**: slow feedback, few comparable casks, the panel as arbiter, and what new tools can and cannot change.
